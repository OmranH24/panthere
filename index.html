The deal

Not here to lose weight. Here to build. Glutes, shoulders, arms. A waist that shows the work.

The scale will barely move. That is the plan. Judge the mirror, the tape, and your gym log.

Train
Four sessions, in order. Miss a day? Do the next one. Never two lower days in a row.

How hard

RIR = reps left in the tank. RIR 2 = two more, not three.

Weeks 1 to 3: stop at RIR 2 to 3. Learn the moves.
Week 4 on: last set to RIR 1. Raises, curls, kickbacks can go all the way.

Notes from Miro

Read this first

You train every day with 5 kg dumbbells. That is why nothing changes.

New rule: four real sessions a week. Heavy. Written down.

Daily gym is optional. Intensity is not.

Week 1: find your numbers

On the first exercise of each day, go up slowly. Find the weight you can do for 8 reps with 2 left in the tank. Write it down.

The four lifts: hip thrust, squat, lat pulldown, incline press.

Send me the four numbers on Sunday.

How to get stronger

Each exercise has a range, like 8 to 10.

Start at 8. Add reps each week. Hit 10 on every set? Add weight. Back to 8.

How much: legs 5 kg. Leg press 10 kg. Upper body 2.5 kg. Dumbbells one size up.

Log every set. No log, no progress.

When you get stuck

Stuck 2 sessions: check sleep and food. Push the last set harder.

Stuck 3 sessions: swap the exercise for a few weeks.

Going backwards everywhere: you are tired. Easy week. Half the sets, 80% weight.

What the scale will do

Month 1: stronger. Waist 1 to 2 cm down. Weight the same.

Month 2 to 4: fuller glutes and shoulders. Firmer arms. Abs start to show.

Month 4 to 6: people notice.

Weight flat but waist smaller and lifts up? It is working. Don't cut food. Don't add cardio. Tell me.

Period week: up to 1.5 kg of water. Normal. Eat a bit more carbs.

Food, simple

1,800 calories. 125 g protein. One thing from each box on Fuel.

Restaurants: protein, salad, one carb. Fries, garlic sauce, bread, hummus: pick one, not four.

Flat white: 120 calories. Caramel frappé: 450. Your call. Just know it.

Hungry all week? Tell me. We add food.

Supplements

Yes: whey, creatine (3 to 5 g a day), vitamin D, coffee.

Creatine adds up to 1 kg of water in the muscle. Not fat. Don't panic.

No: fat burners, BCAAs, pre-workout powders, detox teas.

Non-negotiables

  • Four sessions a week, in order.
  • Log every set.
  • 110 g protein minimum. Every day.
  • Weigh daily. Judge weekly.
  • 8 to 10k steps. Keep your walks.
  • Seven hours of sleep.
  • From week 4, last set near failure.
  • Photos every two weeks.
Red lines
