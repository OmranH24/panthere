I think it happens a little differently.

On the way back we make some stupid bet about something neither of us is willing to admit we’re wrong about, and somehow that becomes your excuse to say, “Fine, come upstairs for one drink.”

Your place is ridiculous, by the way. Sea view, huge windows, everything perfectly put together. Very Panthère.

But instead of doing anything dramatic, we end up on the balcony with another joint, music playing quietly in the background, talking for hours.

And the conversation gets weirdly real.

We talk about turning points in our lives, things we regret, stupid decisions that somehow changed everything, old versions of ourselves we miss, places we want to disappear to one day.....then five minutes later we’re arguing about something completely stupid and laughing our asses off.

At some point you put on a song, look at me completely seriously and go:

“You know, you kinda look gay anyway.”

I just stare at you.

“What the fuck does that even mean?”

You laugh.

Then somehow you make the same joke again two conversations later.

And again.

By the fourth time I’m already buzzed, laughing but getting irritated.

“Panthère…”

“What?”

“Say that one more fucking time.”

Obviously you do.

So I finally get up, walk over to you, catch you gently by the jaw and pull you closer.

“Khalas, u better shut the fuck up.”

And suddenly neither of us is laughing anymore.

I’m looking straight into your eyes, close enough to feel that the whole mood just changed.

And I smile.

“Or keep talking… and find out what happens when you annoy Miro enough.”

That’s probably the first moment of the night where I stop wondering what Panthère is like in private…

and start wanting to find out.
